ABOUT THE STUDIO

A clearer view
of the whole picture.

INAUBU is a concept for bringing inventory visibility and sales insight into one focused automotive experience.

OUR POINT OF VIEW

Clarity is a
competitive edge.

Vehicle businesses work with many moving parts. The right information should be easy to find, understand, and act on.

This concept explores a straightforward way to present vehicle stock, sales snapshots, and performance indicators without overwhelming the people who use them.

THE PRINCIPLE

Details matter.
Perspective matters more.

From a single vehicle record to a wider sales trend, each view should help answer a practical business question.

WHAT GUIDES US

Built on useful
principles.

Make it clear

Prioritize hierarchy, readable information, and a direct route to key details.

Keep it connected

Present stock, sales, and reporting as related parts of one business picture.

Design for decisions

Use metrics and visual summaries to help people ask better questions.

Be transparent

Clearly distinguish demonstration data from verified, live business information.
